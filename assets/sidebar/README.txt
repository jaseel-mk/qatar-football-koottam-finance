PLAYER IMAGE SETUP

Upload your own licensed/private images here using these exact filenames:

messi.jpg
ronaldo.jpg
neymar.jpg
yamal.jpg
chhetri.jpg

The sidebar automatically rotates every 15 seconds.

If a file is not present, the sidebar falls back to:
stadium-fallback.svg

Recommended image shape:
- Portrait or 4:5 crop
- At least 800 px high
- Keep the player's face/body near the center
- Dark or neutral backgrounds work best

The maroon overlay in styles.css blends the photos into the sidebar theme.

To change the interval:
Open app.js and edit:
SIDEBAR_FEATURE_INTERVAL = 15000

To change the captions:
Edit SIDEBAR_FEATURES in app.js.
